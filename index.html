Welcome To CDA

At CDA, we offer any level of protection you may want, from door alarms and motion detectors to complex, state-of-the-art closed circuit televisions.

We make it easy for you to select the right security program, too. A CDA security consultant will show you the three levels of security – Basic, Standard, And Enhanced – and discuss the benefits of each. Once you decide which level makes you most comfortable, CDA will work with you to customize a system to your exact needs.
